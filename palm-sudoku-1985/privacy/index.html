Palm Sudoku 1985 Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Overview

Curry Co LLC (“Curry Co,” “we,” or “us”) publishes Palm Sudoku 1985. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

  • Game data: Puzzle progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase history: If you buy or restore Palm Sudoku 1985 Pro, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information so the app can validate the transaction, unlock Pro features, restore access, prevent fraud, and provide aggregate purchase analytics.
  • Anonymous purchase identifier: The app does not require an account. RevenueCat generates a random, anonymous app-user identifier for purchase management. We do not connect that identifier to your name, email address, or other contact information.

Curry Co does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple processes payments through your Apple Account.

Radio streams and third-party services

The optional JAMS feature connects your device to 181.fm to stream live radio. The stream may include advertising. 181.fm’s servers and service providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and device or connection information under the 181.fm Privacy Policy.

Purchase services are provided by Apple and RevenueCat. Their privacy policies describe their respective practices.

How information is used

Purchase information is used only for app functionality, transaction validation, fraud prevention, customer support, and aggregate purchase analytics. Curry Co does not sell personal information and does not use purchase information to track you across apps or websites for advertising.

Data retention and choices

You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records as needed to provide purchases, comply with law, prevent fraud, and maintain financial records. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings and restore eligible purchases from within the app.

To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information associated with Palm Sudoku 1985, email us at jason@curry.co. We may need information from your purchase receipt to locate an anonymous purchase record.

Children

Palm Sudoku 1985 is not directed specifically to children under 13. The app does not request a child’s name, email address, or other contact information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
